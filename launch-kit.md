# ipullupmqt — launch kit

Everything here is a draft to edit, not a script to obey. Swap in the real
numbers, the real colour of the truck, and your own voice where mine doesn't fit.

**Before anything goes public:** `[confirm insurance/licensing]` — talk to your
insurance agent about carrying paying passengers and about a vehicle used
commercially, and check what the State of Michigan requires for driving people
for money. Nothing in this kit claims you are covered or licensed, and nothing
should be published that does until you have it in writing.

## 5. Outreach message

Short, no pitch deck, no emoji wall. Send it as a DM or a text, one at a time,
to wedding planners, photographers, venues and salons.

> Hey [NAME] — I'm [YOUR NAME], I run ipullupmqt here in Marquette. It's an
> AMG G63 I bring out for weddings, prom send-offs and photo sessions.
>
> I'm reaching out because your couples/clients are exactly who it's built for —
> arrivals, the getaway, or just a better backdrop for portraits.
>
> Two things I'd offer: I'll do your next session or wedding at cost so you can
> see how it shoots, and if it works for your clients I'll pay a referral on
> anything that comes from you.
>
> Want me to send the rates, or just bring it by so you can see it in person?

**Who to send it to first (Marquette):** wedding photographers with public
Instagram accounts, the venues that host receptions out toward the lake and in
the county, the planners who show up tagged in local wedding posts, senior photo
photographers before September, and the salons and dress shops that see prom
traffic in March and April.

## 7. First 30 days

Seasonal reality first: this launches into whatever season you are actually in.
Wedding enquiries cluster from January through spring for summer dates; prom
sells in March and April; senior photos sell in July and August for fall
sessions; the color season around late September and early October is the
busiest photo fortnight of the year in Marquette. Aim the week-3 and week-4
outreach at whichever of those is closest ahead of you.

**Week 1 — exist**
- Claim the handle everywhere: Instagram, TikTok, Facebook page, Google Business Profile.
- Shoot the truck properly once: Presque Isle, the ore dock, Black Rocks, and one clean downtown frame. Golden hour, one afternoon, 40 usable photos.
- Post the launch caption. Pin it.
- Tell every person you already know, individually. This is the highest-conversion hour of the whole month.

**Week 2 — proof**
- Do two free or at-cost sessions with local photographers in exchange for the photos and a tag. Their audience is your customer and their images are your portfolio.
- Post every day: the truck somewhere recognizably Marquette, one detail shot, one behind-the-scenes.
- Add the Google Business Profile photos — locals search "[thing] Marquette" constantly.

**Week 3 — reach**
- Send the outreach message to 25 businesses, one at a time, personalized. Photographers, venues, planners, salons, the dress shops.
- Offer the first five bookings an honest launch rate. Say it is the launch rate and that it ends, then actually end it.
- Post a prom/wedding-specific piece aimed at the nearest season.

**Week 4 — close**
- Follow up once with everyone who did not answer. One follow-up, politely, then leave it.
- Ask anyone who booked for a photo and one sentence you can quote.
- Book one collab with a local restaurant or brewery: you shoot content on their patio, they post it, both audiences see it.
- Count what actually produced enquiries and do more of that next month.

**Free levers worth more than ads here:** the Marquette and NMU Facebook groups
(read the rules first, most ban straight advertising but allow it when someone
asks for recommendations), tagging locations on every post, replying to local
wedding and photography posts as a person rather than an ad, and being parked
where people are. A G63 outside a busy Saturday farmers market is its own
billboard.

**Paid, if you spend anything:** $50–100 of Instagram promotion aimed at a
25-mile radius around Marquette, targeted at engaged couples, beats any print
ad in this market. Do not spend it until the page has a dozen good posts to land on.

## 8. FAQ

Also on the site. The short versions:

**Booking** — message the date, the times, and where it starts and ends. Dates
hold on deposit, not on conversation.

**Deposit** — $100 holds any date and comes off the total. Balance due the day
of, before you roll. Wedding packages booked 90+ days out can split it.

**Cancellations** — more than 14 days out, the deposit moves to another date
within the year. Inside 14 days it is kept, because that date has been turning
away other bookings.

**Weather** — snow and rain are not cancellations in the U.P. Genuinely unsafe
conditions get rescheduled at no cost.

**Who drives** — you do, every time. No self-drive rental today.

**Insurance and licensing** — `[confirm insurance/licensing]`. Do not put
anything specific in writing, on the site, or in a DM until your agent and the
state have confirmed it. This is the one item on this list that can cost you
more than a bad month.

**In the vehicle** — sealed drinks fine, no open containers, no smoking, no
confetti or glitter. Cleaning charge for anything that stains.
