# ipullupmqt

Landing page for a Mercedes-AMG G63 experience business in Marquette, Michigan —
weddings, prom send-offs, photo and video sessions, and brand content across the
Upper Peninsula.

- `index.html` — the site. One static file, no build step.
- `launch-kit.md` — taglines, bios, captions, outreach message, 30-day launch plan.

## Before it goes live

Placeholders to fill, all in `index.html`:

- `[PHONE]` and `[EMAIL]` in the booking section
- the Instagram link, if the handle differs
- `[confirm insurance/licensing]` in the pricing note and the FAQ — the site
  deliberately makes no claim about coverage or licensing, and should not until
  it is confirmed in writing
- the G63's colour, if you want it named in the copy
- `images/hero.jpg` — drop a wide shot of the truck in and the hero uses it
  automatically. Until then the gradient stands in and nothing looks broken.
