About

Built for the U.P.

ipullupmqt is a Mercedes-AMG G63 and the guy who drives it, working out of Marquette and up and down the Upper Peninsula. It started the way most good ideas here do — people kept asking to take pictures with the truck, so I started saying yes on purpose.

Now it shows up for weddings at the venues out by the lake, prom send-offs on the front lawn, senior sessions at Presque Isle and the Black Rocks, and content days with local spots that deserve a better backdrop than a parking lot. It handles the Marquette winter the way a G-Wagon should, so December bookings are as real as July ones.

One truck, one driver, one thing done well: making the moment you already planned feel like an event. Bring the date, I'll bring the arrival.

Before you book

How do I book a date?

Message me with the date, the times, and where it starts and ends. I'll confirm whether it's open and send the total. Dates hold once the deposit is in, not before.

What's the deposit?

$100 holds any date, applied to your total. The balance is due the day of, before we roll. Wedding packages booked more than 90 days out can split the balance.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancel more than 14 days out and the deposit moves to another date within the year. Inside 14 days it's kept, since that date is usually turning away other bookings by then.

What about weather?

It's a G-Wagon in the U.P. — snow and rain are not cancellations. If conditions are genuinely unsafe, we reschedule and nothing is lost.

Who drives?

I do, on every booking. Self-drive rental isn't offered right now. On photo and video sessions I'm on site with the vehicle the whole time.
